\# Day 01 - DL Frameworks 🚀



\## TensorFlow

\- Google’s deep learning framework

\- Used for building \& training neural networks

\- Good for production



\## Keras

\- High-level API on top of TensorFlow

\- Simple and beginner-friendly

\- Less code, faster modeling



\## PyTorch

\- Developed by Meta (Facebook)

\- Popular in research

\- Easy debugging, Python-like style



\## Key Difference

\- TensorFlow → Production

\- Keras → Simplicity

\- PyTorch → Research
